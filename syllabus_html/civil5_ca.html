Structural Design-I [101501]

3-1-0-4 (as printed alongside the course heading)

The Semester-V summary table lists L-T-P as 3-0-0 and 3 credits; the detailed course heading prints “3104”. Both source entries are preserved here without reconciling the discrepancy.

Unit-1.06 hrs

Fundamentals of RC Design and the Working Stress Method:

This unit introduces the basic material properties of concrete and steel, and the foundational design concepts using the elastic theory. It covers the traditional working stress method (WSM) and its application to different types of beam sections.

  • Concepts of Reinforced Concrete: Introduction to RC, stress-strain characteristics of concrete and steel, and the elastic theory.
  • Working Stress Method: Design philosophy and assumptions of the method.
  • Singly Reinforced Beam Analysis and Design: Analysis and design of balanced, under-reinforced, and over-reinforced rectangular sections using the WSM.
Unit-2.09 hrs

Limit State Method: Principles and Design of Beams:

This unit transitions to the modern limit state method (LSM) of design. It explains the core concepts of LSM and applies them to the design of various beam types for flexure, shear, and torsion.

  • Concepts of Limit State Method: Principles of probability and reliability, characteristic loads and strengths, partial safety factors for loads and materials, and the different limit states of collapse and serviceability.
  • Design of Beams for Flexure: Design of singly reinforced, doubly reinforced, rectangular, and T-sections.
  • Design for Shear and Bond: Principles of shear resistance, design of shear reinforcement, and ensuring proper bond and anchorage.
  • Design for Bending and Torsion: Design of beams subjected to combined bending and torsion as per IS Code recommendations.
Unit-3.07 hrs

Design of Compression Members (Columns):

This unit focuses on the design of columns, which are critical elements for supporting axial and bending loads. It covers short and slender columns under various loading conditions.

  • Design for Axial Loads: Design of short columns subjected to direct compression.
  • Design for Uni-axial Bending: Design of columns subjected to combined axial load and uni-axial bending moment as per IS Code methods.
  • Design for Bi-axial Bending: Design of columns for axial load and bi-axial bending moment.
Unit-4.06 hrs

Design of Slabs and Staircases:

This unit covers the design of common slab systems and different types of staircases. It includes the design of one-way and two-way slabs, as well as complex slab systems like flat slabs.

  • Design of Slabs: Design of one-way and two-way slabs.
  • Design of Flat Slabs: Design considerations for flat slabs.
  • Design of Staircases: Design of dog-legged and open-well type staircases.
Unit-5.06 hrs

Design of Foundations:

This unit addresses the design of various types of concrete foundations for transferring building loads to the supporting soil.

  • Design of Isolated Footings: Design of square and rectangular isolated footings subjected to axial load and moments.
  • Design of Combined Foundations: Design of combined footings for supporting multiple columns.
Unit-6.08 hrs

Prestressed Concrete:

This unit introduces the advanced topic of prestressed concrete. It covers the fundamental principles, materials, prestressing methods, and the analysis of prestressed members.

  • Principles of Prestressed Concrete: Basic principles, materials, and methods of prestressing (pre-tensioning and post-tensioning).
  • Losses in Prestress: Analysis of various types of losses, including elastic shortening, creep, shrinkage, and friction.
  • Analysis of Prestressed Sections: Analysis of various types of sections (rectangular, T-section, box section, and girders) subjected to prestress and external loads.
  • Design of Slabs: Design of post-tensioned slabs.

Professional Practice, Law & Ethics

3-0-0-0 (printed alongside course heading)

Unit-1.08 hrs

Professional Practice:

  • Respective roles of various stakeholders: Government (constituting regulatory bodies and standardization organizations, prescribing norms to ensure safety of the citizens).
  • Standardization Bodies (ex. BIS, IRC) (formulating standards of practice).
  • Professional bodies (ex. Institution of Engineers(India), Indian Roads Congress, IIA/ COA, ECI, Local Bodies/ Planning Authorities) (certifying professionals and offering platforms for interaction).
  • Clients/ owners (role governed by contracts).
  • Developers (role governed by regulations such as RERA).
  • Consultants (role governed by bodies such as CEAI).
  • Contractors (role governed by contracts and regulatory Acts and Standards).
  • Manufacturers/ Vendors/ Service agencies (role governed by contracts and regulatory Acts and Standards).
Unit-2.05 hrs

Professional Ethics:

  • Definition of Ethics, Professional Ethics, Business Ethics, Corporate Ethics, Engineering Ethics, Personal Ethics.
  • Code of Ethics as defined in the website of Institution of Engineers (India).
  • Profession, Professionalism, Professional Responsibility, Professional Ethics.
  • Conflict of Interest, Gift Vs Bribery, Environmental breaches, Negligence, Deficiencies in state-of-the-art.
  • Vigil Mechanism, Whistleblowing, protected disclosures.
Unit-3.09 hrs

General Principles of Contracts Management:

  • Indian Contract Act, 1972 and amendments covering General principles of contracting.
  • Contract Formation & Law; Privacy of contract.
  • Various types of contract and their features; Valid & Voidable Contracts.
  • Prime and sub-contracts; Joint Ventures & Consortium.
  • Complex contract terminology; Tenders, Request For Proposals, Bids & Proposals; Bid Evaluation.
  • Contract Conditions & Specifications; Critical /“Red Flag” conditions.
  • Contract award & Notice To Proceed; Variations & Changes in Contracts; Differing site conditions.
  • Cost escalation; Delays, Suspensions & Terminations; Time extensions & Force Majeure; Delay Analysis.
  • Liquidated damages & Penalties; Insurance & Taxation.
  • Performance and Excusable Non-performance; Contract documentation; Contract Notices.
  • Wrong practices in contracting (Bid shopping, Bid fixing, Cartels); Reverse auction; Case Studies.
  • Build-Own-Operate & variations; Public- Private Partnerships; International Commercial Terms.
Unit-4.07 hrs

Arbitration, Conciliation and ADR (Alternative Dispute Resolution) system:

  • Arbitration – meaning, scope and types – distinction between laws of 1940 and 1996.
  • UNCITRAL model law – Arbitration and expert determination; Extent of judicial intervention.
  • International commercial arbitration.
  • Arbitration agreements – essential and kinds, validity, reference and interim measures by court.
  • Arbitration tribunal – appointment, challenge, jurisdiction of arbitral tribunal, powers, grounds of challenge, procedure and court assistance.
  • Award including Form and content, Grounds for setting aside an award, Enforcement, Appeal and Revision.
  • Enforcement of foreign awards – New York and Geneva Convention Awards.
  • Distinction between conciliation, negotiation, mediation and arbitration, confidentiality, resort to judicial proceedings, costs.
  • Dispute Resolution Boards; Lok Adalats.
Unit-5.05 hrs

Engagement of Labour and Labour & other construction-related Laws:

  • Role of Labour in Civil Engineering.
  • Methods of engaging labour- on rolls, labour sub-contract, piece rate work.
  • Industrial Disputes Act, 1947; Collective bargaining.
  • Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946.
  • Workmen’s Compensation Act, 1923.
  • Building & Other Construction Workers (regulation of employment and conditions of service) Act (1996) and Rules (1998).
  • RERA Act 2017, NBC 2017.
Unit-6.08 hrs

Law relating to Intellectual property:

  • Introduction – meaning of intellectual property, main forms of IP, Copyright, Trademarks, Patents and Designs, Secrets.
  • Law relating to Copyright in India including Historical evolution of Copy Rights Act, 1957.
  • Meaning of copyright – computer programs, Ownership of copyrights and assignment, Criteria of infringement.
  • Piracy in Internet – Remedies and procedures in India.
  • Law relating to Patents under Patents Act, 1970 including Concept and historical perspective of patents law in India.
  • Patentable inventions with special reference to biotechnology products; Patent protection for computer programs.
  • Process of obtaining patent – application, examination, opposition and sealing of patents.
  • Patent cooperation treaty and grounds for opposition; Rights and obligations of patentee.
  • Duration of patents – law and policy considerations, Infringement and related remedies.
