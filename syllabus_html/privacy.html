Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

BEU Connect is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

BEU Connect is designed to be a 100% private application. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All data you enter, such as attendance records or GPA calculations, is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

AI Doubt Solver

When you use the AI Doubt Solver, your queries are processed by Google's Gemini API. No personal identifiers are sent with these queries. We recommend not sharing personal information in your academic questions.

Analytics

We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools that monitor your behavior within the app.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to our development team via the official Telegram channel.
