Structural Design-I [101501]

Unit 1 · Fundamentals of RC Design and the Working Stress Method

Fundamentals of RC Design and the Working Stress Method: This unit introduces the basic material properties of concrete and steel, and the foundational design concepts using the elastic theory. It covers the traditional working stress method (WSM) and its application to different types of beam sections.

Key Concepts

Concepts of Reinforced Concrete: Introduction to RC, stress-strain characteristics of concrete and steel, and the elastic theory.
Working Stress Method: Design philosophy and assumptions of the method.
Singly Reinforced Beam Analysis and Design: Analysis and design of balanced, under- reinforced, and over-reinforced rectangular sections using the WSM.

Unit 2 · Limit State Method

Limit State Method: Principles and Design of Beams: This unit transitions to the modern limit state method (LSM) of design. It explains the core concepts of LSM and applies them to the design of various beam types for flexure, shear, and torsion.

Topics 1–3

Concepts of Limit State Method: Principles of probability and reliability, characteristic loads and strengths, partial safety factors for loads and materials, and the different limit states of collapse and serviceability.
Design of Beams for Flexure: Design of singly reinforced, doubly reinforced, rectangular, and T-sections.
Design for Shear and Bond: Principles of shear resistance, design of shear reinforcement, and ensuring proper bond and anchorage.

Topics 4–4

Design for Bending and Torsion: Design of beams subjected to combined bending and torsion as per IS Code recommendations.

Unit 3 · Design of Compression Members (Columns)

Design of Compression Members (Columns): This unit focuses on the design of columns, which are critical elements for supporting axial and bending loads. It covers short and slender columns under various loading conditions.

Key Concepts

Design for Axial Loads: Design of short columns subjected to direct compression.
Design for Uni-axial Bending: Design of columns subjected to combined axial load and uni- axial bending moment as per IS Code methods.
Design for Bi-axial Bending: Design of columns for axial load and bi-axial bending moment.

Unit 4 · Design of Slabs and Staircases

Design of Slabs and Staircases: This unit covers the design of common slab systems and different types of staircases.

Topics 1–3

It includes the design of one-way and two-way slabs, as well as complex slab systems like flat slabs.
Design of Slabs: Design of one-way and two-way slabs.
Design of Flat Slabs: Design considerations for flat slabs.

Topics 4–4

Design of Staircases: Design of dog-legged and open-well type staircases.

Unit 5 · Design of Foundations

Design of Foundations: This unit addresses the design of various types of concrete foundations for transferring building loads to the supporting soil.

Key Concepts

Design of Isolated Footings: Design of square and rectangular isolated footings subjected to axial load and moments.
Design of Combined Foundations: Design of combined footings for supporting multiple columns.

Unit 6 · Prestressed Concrete

Prestressed Concrete: This unit introduces the advanced topic of prestressed concrete. It covers the fundamental principles, materials, prestressing methods, and the analysis of prestressed members.

Topics 1–3

Principles of Prestressed Concrete: Basic principles, materials, and methods of prestressing (pre-tensioning and post-tensioning).
Losses in Prestress: Analysis of various types of losses, including elastic shortening, creep, shrinkage, and friction.
Analysis of Prestressed Sections: Analysis of various types of sections (rectangular, T- section, box section, and girders) subjected to prestress and external loads.

Topics 4–4

Design of Slabs: Design of post-tensioned slabs.
