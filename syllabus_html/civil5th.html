Structural Design-I [101501]

Unit-1.0 6 hrs

Fundamentals of RC Design and the Working Stress Method:

This unit introduces the basic material properties of concrete and steel, and the foundational

design concepts using the elastic theory. It covers the traditional working stress method

(WSM) and its application to different types of beam sections.

Concepts of Reinforced Concrete: Introduction to RC, stress-strain characteristics of

concrete and steel, and the elastic theory.

Working Stress Method: Design philosophy and assumptions of the method.

Singly Reinforced Beam Analysis and Design: Analysis and design of balanced, under-

reinforced, and over-reinforced rectangular sections using the WSM.

Unit-2.0 9 hrs

Limit State Method: Principles and Design of Beams:

This unit transitions to the modern limit state method (LSM) of design. It explains the core

concepts of LSM and applies them to the design of various beam types for flexure, shear, and

torsion.

Concepts of Limit State Method: Principles of probability and reliability, characteristic

loads and strengths, partial safety factors for loads and materials, and the different limit states

of collapse and serviceability.

Design of Beams for Flexure: Design of singly reinforced, doubly reinforced, rectangular,

and T-sections.

Design for Shear and Bond: Principles of shear resistance, design of shear reinforcement,

and ensuring proper bond and anchorage.

Design for Bending and Torsion: Design of beams subjected to combined bending and

torsion as per IS Code recommendations.

Unit-3.0 7 hrs

Design of Compression Members (Columns):

This unit focuses on the design of columns, which are critical elements for supporting axial

and bending loads. It covers short and slender columns under various loading conditions.

Design for Axial Loads: Design of short columns subjected to direct compression.

Design for Uni-axial Bending: Design of columns subjected to combined axial load and uni-

axial bending moment as per IS Code methods.

Design for Bi-axial Bending: Design of columns for axial load and bi-axial bending

moment.

Unit-4.0 6 hrs

Design of Slabs and Staircases:

This unit covers the design of common slab systems and different types of staircases. It

includes the design of one-way and two-way slabs, as well as complex slab systems like flat

slabs.

Design of Slabs: Design of one-way and two-way slabs.

Design of Flat Slabs: Design considerations for flat slabs.

Design of Staircases: Design of dog-legged and open-well type staircases.

Unit-5.0: 6 hrs

Design of Foundations:

This unit addresses the design of various types of concrete foundations for transferring

building loads to the supporting soil.

Design of Isolated Footings: Design of square and rectangular isolated footings subjected to

axial load and moments.

Design of Combined Foundations: Design of combined footings for supporting multiple

columns.

Unit-6.0 8 hrs

Prestressed Concrete:

This unit introduces the advanced topic of prestressed concrete. It covers the fundamental

principles, materials, prestressing methods, and the analysis of prestressed members.

Principles of Prestressed Concrete: Basic principles, materials, and methods of prestressing

(pre-tensioning and post-tensioning).

Losses in Prestress: Analysis of various types of losses, including elastic shortening, creep,

shrinkage, and friction.

Analysis of Prestressed Sections: Analysis of various types of sections (rectangular, T-

section, box section, and girders) subjected to prestress and external loads.

Design of Slabs: Design of post-tensioned slabs.

and Financial System, Central Bank –Monetary Aggregates; Commercial Banks & their

Hydrology and Water Resources Engineering [101504]

Unit- 1.0 5 hrs

Introduction - hydrologic cycle, water-budget equation, history of hydrology, world water

balance, applications in engineering, sources of data. Precipitation - forms of precipitation,

characteristics of precipitation in India, measurement of precipitation, rain gauge network,

mean precipitation over an area, deptharea-duration relationships, maximum intensity/depth-

duration-frequency relationship, Probable Maximum Precipitation (PMP), rainfall data in

India.

Unit- 2.0 5 hrs

Abstractions from precipitation - evaporation process, evaporimeters, analytical methods of

evaporation estimation, reservoir evaporation and methods for its reduction,

evapotranspiration, measurement of evapotranspiration, evapotranspiration equations,

potential evapotranspiration over India, actual evapotranspiration, interception, depression

storage, infiltration, infiltration capacity, measurement of infiltration, modelling infiltration

capacity, classification of infiltration capacities, infiltration indices.

Unit- 3.0 3 hrs

Runoff - runoff volume, SCS-CN method of estimating runoff volume, flowduration curve,

flow-mass curve, hydrograph, factors affecting runoff hydrograph, components of

hydrograph, base flow separation, effective rainfall, unit hydrograph surface water resources

of India, environmental flows.

Unit- 4.0 6 hrs

Ground water and well hydrology - forms of subsurface water, saturated formation, aquifer

properties, geologic formations of aquifers, well hydraulics: steady state flow in wells,

equilibrium equations for confined and unconfined aquifers, aquifer tests. Water withdrawals

and uses – water for energy production, water for agriculture, water for hydroelectric

generation; flood control. Analysis of surface water supply, Water requirement of crops-

Crops and crop seasons in India, cropping pattern, duty and delta; Quality of irrigation water;

Soil-water relationships, root zone soil water, infiltration, consumptive use, irrigation

requirement, frequency of irrigation; Methods of applying water to the fields: surface, sub-

surface, sprinkler and trickle / drip irrigation.

Unit- 5.0 4 hrs

Distribution systems - canal systems, alignment of canals, canal losses, estimation of design

discharge. Design of channels- rigid boundary channels, alluvial channels, Kennedy’s and

Lacey’s theory of regime channels. Canal outlets: non-modular, semi-modular and modula.r

outlets. Water logging: causes, effects and remedial measures. Lining of canals, types of

lining. Drainage of irrigated lands: necessity, methods.

Unit- 6.0 5 hrs

Dams and spillways - embankment dams: Classification, design considerations, estimation

and control of seepage, slope protection. Gravity dams: forces on gravity dams, causes of

failure, stress analysis, elementary and practical profile. Arch and buttress dams. Spillways:

components of spillways, types of gates for spillway crests; Reservoirs- Types, capacity of

reservoirs, yield of reservoir, reservoir regulation, sedimentation, economic height of dam,

selection of suitable site.

Professional Practice, Law & Ethics [101505]

Unit- 1.0 8 hrs

Professional Practice: Respective roles of various stakeholders: Government (constituting

regulatory bodies and standardization organizations, prescribing norms to ensure safety of the

citizens); Standardization Bodies (ex. BIS, IRC)(formulating standards of practice);

professional bodies (ex. Institution of Engineers(India), Indian Roads Congress, IIA/ COA,

ECI, Local Bodies/ Planning Authorities) (certifying professionals and offering platforms for

interaction); Clients/ owners (role governed by contracts); Developers (role governed by

regulations such as RERA); Consultants (role governed by bodies such as CEAI); Contractors

(role governed by contracts and regulatory Acts and Standards); Manufacturers/ Vendors/

Service agencies (role governed by contracts and regulatory Acts and Standards)

Unit- 2.0 5 hrs

Professional Ethics: Definition of Ethics, Professional Ethics, Business Ethics, Corporate

Ethics, Engineering Ethics, Personal Ethics; Code of Ethics as defined in the website of

Institution of Engineers (India); Profession, Professionalism, Professional Responsibility,

Professional Ethics; Conflict of Interest, Gift Vs Bribery, Environmental breaches,

Negligence, Deficiencies in state-of-the-art; Vigil Mechanism, Whistleblowing, protected

disclosures.

Unit- 3.0 9 hrs

General Principles of Contracts Management: Indian Contract Act, 1972 and amendments

covering General principles of contracting; Contract Formation & Law; Privacy of contract;

Various types of contract and their features; Valid & Voidable Contracts; Prime and sub-

contracts; Joint Ventures & Consortium; Complex contract terminology; Tenders, Request

For Proposals, Bids & Proposals; Bid Evaluation; Contract Conditions & Specifications;

Critical /“Red Flag” conditions; Contract award & Notice To Proceed; Variations & Changes

in Contracts; Differing site conditions; Cost escalation; Delays, Suspensions & Terminations;

Time extensions & Force Majeure; Delay Analysis; Liquidated damages & Penalties;

Insurance & Taxation; Performance and Excusable Non-performance; Contract

documentation; Contract Notices; Wrong practices in contracting (Bid shopping, Bid fixing,

Cartels); Reverse auction; Case Studies; Build-Own-Operate & variations; Public- Private

Partnerships; International Commercial Terms;

Unit- 4.0 7 hrs

Arbitration, Conciliation and ADR (Alternative Dispute Resolution) system: Arbitration

– meaning, scope and types – distinction between laws of 1940 and 1996; UNCITRAL model

law – Arbitration and expert determination; Extent of judicial intervention; International

commercial arbitration; Arbitration agreements – essential and kinds, validity, reference and

programs, Process of obtaining patent – application, examination, opposition and sealing of

patents, Patent cooperation treaty and grounds for opposition, Rights and obligations of

patentee, Duration of patents – law and policy considerations, Infringement and related

remedies;
