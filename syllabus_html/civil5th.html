Theory Subjects

101501 · 3 CREDITS
Structural Design-I
Lecture: 3 · ESE: 70 · IA: 30
⌄
Unit 1 · Fundamentals of RC Design and Working Stress Method 6 hrs
Concepts of Reinforced Concrete: Introduction to RC, stress-strain characteristics of concrete and steel, and elastic theory. Working Stress Method: Design philosophy and assumptions of the method. Singly Reinforced Beam Analysis and Design: Analysis and design of balanced, under-reinforced, and over-reinforced rectangular sections using WSM.
Unit 2 · Limit State Method: Principles and Design of Beams 9 hrs
Concepts of Limit State Method: Principles of probability and reliability, characteristic loads and strengths, partial safety factors for loads and materials, and limit states of collapse and serviceability. Design of Beams for Flexure: Singly reinforced, doubly reinforced, rectangular and T-sections. Design for Shear and Bond: Shear resistance, shear reinforcement, bond and anchorage. Design for Bending and Torsion: Beams subjected to combined bending and torsion as per IS Code recommendations.
Unit 3 · Design of Compression Members (Columns) 7 hrs
Design for Axial Loads: Short columns subjected to direct compression. Design for Uni-axial Bending: Columns subjected to combined axial load and uni-axial bending moment. Design for Bi-axial Bending: Columns subjected to axial load and bi-axial bending moment.
Unit 4 · Design of Slabs and Staircases 6 hrs
Design of one-way and two-way slabs. Design of flat slabs: Design considerations. Design of staircases: Dog-legged and open-well type staircases.
Unit 5 · Design of Foundations 6 hrs
Design of Isolated Footings: Square and rectangular isolated footings subjected to axial load and moments. Design of Combined Foundations: Combined footings for supporting multiple columns.
Unit 6 · Prestressed Concrete 8 hrs
Principles of Prestressed Concrete: Basic principles, materials, pre-tensioning and post-tensioning. Losses in Prestress: Elastic shortening, creep, shrinkage and friction. Analysis of Prestressed Sections: Rectangular, T-section, box section and girders subjected to prestress and external loads. Design of post-tensioned slabs.
101502 · 4 CREDITS
Environmental Engineering-I
Lecture: 3 · Tutorial: 1 · ESE: 70 · IA: 30
⌄
Unit 1 · Water Supply 10 hrs
Sources of Water and quality issues, water quality requirements for different beneficial uses, water quality standards, water quality indices, water safety plans. Water Supply Systems: Need for planned water supply schemes, industrial and agricultural water requirements, components of water supply system, transmission of water, distribution system, various valves used in water supply systems, service reservoirs and design.
Unit 2 · Water Treatment 5 hrs
Aeration, sedimentation, coagulation, flocculation, filtration, disinfection and advanced treatments like adsorption, ion exchange and membrane processes.
Unit 3 · Air Pollution 7 hrs
Composition and properties of air, quantification of air pollutants, monitoring of air pollutants, occupational hazards, urban air pollution, automobile pollution and chemistry of combustion.
Unit 4 · Air Quality and Control 7 hrs
Automobile engines, quality of fuel, operating conditions and interrelationship. Air quality standards, control measures for air pollution, construction and limitations.
Unit 5 · Noise Pollution 5 hrs
Basic concept of noise, measurement and various control methods.
Unit 6 · Building Plumbing 8 hrs
Introduction to various types of home plumbing systems for water supply and wastewater disposal, high-rise building plumbing, pressure reducing valves, break pressure tanks, storage tanks, building drainage for high-rise buildings and various kinds of fixtures and fittings used.
101503 · 2 CREDITS
Engineering Economics, Estimation & Costing
Lecture: 2 · ESE: 70 · IA: 30
⌄
Unit 1 · Principles of Economics 8 hrs
Basic principles and methodology of economics. Demand and supply, elasticity, government policies and applications. Theory of the firm and market structure. Basic macro-economic concepts including GDP, GNP, NI and disposable income. Aggregate demand and supply (IS/LM), price indices (WPI/CPI), interest rates, direct and indirect taxes. Public sector economics, welfare, externalities, labour market, monetary and financial systems, central bank, commercial banks, capital and debt markets, monetary and fiscal policy, inflation and Phillips Curve.
Unit 2 · Business and Managerial Economics 7 hrs
Elements of business/managerial economics and forms of organizations. Cost and cost control, types of costs, lifecycle costs, budgets, break-even analysis, capital budgeting, linear programming. Investment analysis: NPV, ROI, IRR, payback period, depreciation and time value of money. Business forecasting, cash flow and financial statements, case study method.
Unit 3 · Indian Economy 4 hrs
Indian economy: Brief overview of post-independence period and plans, post-reform growth, structure of productive activity. Issues of inclusion across sectors, states/regions and groups of people, urbanization. Employment: Informal, organized, unorganized, public and private sectors. Challenges and policy debates in monetary, fiscal, social and external sectors.
Unit 4 · Estimation and Measurements 9 hrs
Introduction to estimation, relevant Indian Standard Specifications, taking out quantities from work requirements, comparison of alternatives, bar bending schedules and mass haul diagrams. Estimating earthwork, foundations, concrete, masonry, finishes, interiors and MEP works. BIM and quantity take-offs, equipment costs, labour costs, rate analysis, material survey, thumb rules for material requirements, percentage breakup of cost, cost sensitive index, market survey and use of computers in quantity surveying.
Unit 5 · Specifications and Rate Analysis 5 hrs
Specifications: Types, requirements and importance, detailed specifications for buildings, roads, minor bridges and industrial structures. Rate analysis: Purpose, importance, necessity, factors affecting rate analysis, task work and daily output/productivity from different equipment.
Unit 6 · Tender and Bidding 9 hrs
Preparation of tender documents, importance of inviting tenders, contract types and relative merits, prequalification, general and special conditions, termination of contracts, extra work and changes, penalty and liquidated charges, settlement of disputes, R.A. Bill and Final Bill, advance payment, insurance, claims and price variation. Preparing bids: Bid price build-up including material, labour, equipment costs, risks, direct and indirect overheads and profits. Bid conditions, alternative specifications, alternative bids and bid process management. Introduction to Acts pertaining to minimum wages, workmen's compensation, contracts, arbitration and easement rights.
101504 · 3 CREDITS
Hydrology and Water Resources Engineering
Lecture: 3 · ESE: 70 · IA: 30
⌄
Unit 1 · Introduction and Precipitation 5 hrs
Hydrologic cycle, water-budget equation, history of hydrology, world water balance, applications in engineering and sources of data. Precipitation: Forms of precipitation, characteristics of precipitation in India, measurement of precipitation, rain gauge network, mean precipitation over an area, depth-area-duration relationships, maximum intensity/depth-duration-frequency relationship, Probable Maximum Precipitation (PMP) and rainfall data in India.
Unit 2 · Abstractions from Precipitation 5 hrs
Evaporation process, evaporimeters, analytical methods of evaporation estimation, reservoir evaporation and methods for its reduction. Evapotranspiration, measurement of evapotranspiration, evapotranspiration equations, potential and actual evapotranspiration. Interception, depression storage, infiltration, infiltration capacity, measurement of infiltration, modelling infiltration capacity, classification of infiltration capacities and infiltration indices.
Unit 3 · Runoff 3 hrs
Runoff volume, SCS-CN method of estimating runoff volume, flow-duration curve, flow-mass curve, hydrograph, factors affecting runoff hydrograph, components of hydrograph, base flow separation, effective rainfall, unit hydrograph, surface water resources of India and environmental flows.
Unit 4 · Groundwater and Irrigation 6 hrs
Forms of subsurface water, saturated formation, aquifer properties, geological formations of aquifers, well hydraulics, steady state flow in wells, equilibrium equations for confined and unconfined aquifers and aquifer tests. Water withdrawals and uses, energy production, agriculture, hydroelectric generation and flood control. Analysis of surface water supply, water requirement of crops, crop seasons in India, cropping pattern, duty and delta, quality of irrigation water, soil-water relationships, root zone soil water, consumptive use, irrigation requirement and frequency of irrigation. Methods of applying water: Surface, subsurface, sprinkler and trickle/drip irrigation.
Unit 5 · Distribution Systems and Canals 4 hrs
Canal systems, alignment of canals, canal losses and estimation of design discharge. Design of channels: Rigid boundary channels, alluvial channels, Kennedy's and Lacey's theory of regime channels. Canal outlets: Non-modular, semi-modular and modular outlets. Water logging: Causes, effects and remedial measures. Lining of canals, types of lining and drainage of irrigated lands.
Unit 6 · Dams and Spillways 5 hrs
Embankment dams: Classification, design considerations, estimation and control of seepage, slope protection. Gravity dams: Forces on gravity dams, causes of failure, stress analysis, elementary and practical profile. Arch and buttress dams. Spillways: Components of spillways and types of gates for spillway crests. Reservoirs: Types, capacity, yield, regulation, sedimentation, economic height of dam and selection of suitable site.
101505 · NON-CREDIT
Professional Practice, Law & Ethics
Lecture: 3 · Non-credit course
⌄
Unit 1 · Professional Practice 8 hrs
Roles of various stakeholders: Government, standardization bodies such as BIS and IRC, professional bodies, clients/owners, developers, consultants, contractors, manufacturers, vendors and service agencies.
Unit 2 · Professional Ethics 5 hrs
Definition of ethics, professional ethics, business ethics, corporate ethics, engineering ethics and personal ethics. Code of Ethics of Institution of Engineers (India), professionalism, professional responsibility, conflict of interest, gift vs bribery, environmental breaches, negligence, vigil mechanism, whistleblowing and protected disclosures.
Unit 3 · Contracts Management 9 hrs
Indian Contract Act, 1972 and amendments, contract formation and law, types of contracts, valid and voidable contracts, prime and sub-contracts, joint ventures and consortium. Tenders, RFPs, bids and proposals, bid evaluation, contract conditions, contract award, variations and changes, cost escalation, delays, suspensions and terminations, force majeure, delay analysis, liquidated damages, insurance, taxation, contract documentation, wrong practices in contracting, PPP and international commercial terms.
Unit 4 · Arbitration and ADR 7 hrs
Arbitration, conciliation and Alternative Dispute Resolution system, arbitration laws of 1940 and 1996, UNCITRAL model law, arbitration agreements, arbitration tribunal, appointment, jurisdiction, powers and procedure. Awards, enforcement, appeal, revision, foreign awards, New York and Geneva Convention Awards, distinction between conciliation, negotiation, mediation and arbitration, Dispute Resolution Boards and Lok Adalats.
Unit 5 · Labour and Construction Laws 5 hrs
Role of labour in Civil Engineering, methods of engaging labour, Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, collective bargaining, Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Building and Other Construction Workers Act, 1996 and Rules, 1998, RERA Act 2017 and NBC 2017.
Unit 6 · Intellectual Property Rights 8 hrs
Meaning of intellectual property and its main forms: Copyright, trademarks, patents, designs and secrets. Copyright law in India, Copyright Act 1957, ownership and assignment, infringement, internet piracy and remedies. Patents Act 1970, patentable inventions, process of obtaining patents, application, examination, opposition, rights and obligations of patentee, duration of patents and infringement remedies.
101506 · NON-CREDIT
Disability, Accessibility and Universal Design
Lecture: 3 · Non-credit course
⌄
Unit 1 · Human Diversity and Inclusion 6 hrs
Understanding concepts of diversity, including vulnerable groups, inclusion, need and significance, and impacts.
Unit 2 · Understanding Disability 5 hrs
Definitions, models and prevalence theory of disability, various concepts and models, and prevalence.
Unit 3 · Disability Types and Environmental Needs 9 hrs
Disability classification, functional limitations and key coping strategies in the environment. Physical and movement disabilities, vision impairments, hearing, speech, cognitive, learning and other disabilities as per the RPWD Act 2016.
Unit 4 · Environmental Barriers 9 hrs
Physical, social and institutional barriers in national and international contexts. Harmonized Guidelines, NBC, international codes and guidelines, accessibility codes and their review. Legislative policies and programs: UNCRPD, RPWD Act 2016, SDGs and urban development programs in India and other countries.
Unit 5 · Universal Design Theory 8 hrs
Evolution from Barrier Free Environment to Universal Design, definitions, associated myths, concepts, terminologies and perspectives. Universal Design Principles: International and Indian principles, UD goals and their criteria.
Unit 6 · Universal Design Case Studies 5 hrs
Built Environment Case Studies from Urban Transportation and other contexts like peri-urban and rural settings.
