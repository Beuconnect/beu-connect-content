BT Biotechnology
Section 1: Engineering Mathematics
Linear Algebra: Matrices and determinants, Systems of linear equations, Eigen values and Eigen vectors.
Calculus: Limits, continuity and differentiability, Partial derivatives, Maxima and minima, Sequences
and series, Test for convergence, Fourier Series.
Differential Equations: Linear and nonlinear first order ODEs, higher order ODEs with constant
coefficients, Cauchy’s and Euler’s equations, Laplace transforms, PDE-Laplace, heat and wave
equations.
Probability and Statistics: Mean, median, mode and standard deviation; Random variables; Poisson,
normal and binomial distributions; Correlation and regression analysis.
Numerical Methods: Solution of linear and nonlinear algebraic equations; Integration of trapezoidal
and Simpson’s rule; Single and multistep methods for differential equations.
Section 2: General Biology
Biochemistry: Biomolecules-structure and functions; Biological membranes, structure, action
potential and transport processes; Enzymes- classification, kinetics and mechanism of action; Basic
metabolic pathways and their regulation (glycolysis, TCA cycle, oxidative phosphorylation,
photosynthesis); Nitrogen fixation and assimilation; Vitamins and coenzymes.
Microbiology: Viruses- structure and classification; Microbial classification and diversity (bacterial,
algal and fungal); Microbial growth and nutrition; Aerobic and anaerobic respiration; Control of
microorganisms; Microbial genetics.
Cell Biology: Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell structure; Cell cycle and cell growth control; Cell-cell
communication, cell signaling and signal transduction.
Section 3: Genetics, Cellular and Molecular Biology
Molecular Biology: Molecular structure of genes and chromosomes; Mutations and mutagenesis;
Nucleic acid replication, transcription, translation and their regulatory mechanisms in prokaryotes and
eukaryotes; Mendelian inheritance; Gene interaction; Complementation; Linkage, recombination and
chromosome mapping; Extra chromosomal inheritance; Microbial genetics (plasmids, transformation,
transduction, conjugation); Horizontal gene transfer and transposable elements; RNA interference;
DNA damage and repair; Chromosomal aberrations; Cytogenetics of cancer.
Section 4: Fundamentals of Biological Engineering
Engineering principles of bioprocesses: Methods of material and energy balances; Mass transfer
and heat transfer concepts; Stoichiometry and design of fermentation processes; Rheology of fluids
and its importance in bioprocesses; Bioreactor design and scale-up, residence time distribution;
Methods of sterilization; Process control (sensors and controllers).
Section 5: Bioprocess Engineering and Process Biotechnology
Bioprocess technology: Kinetics of microbial growth, substrate utilization and product formation;
Medium formulation and optimization; Sterilization of air and media; Batch, fed-batch and
continuous processes; Design and operation of various types of bioreactors (stirred tank, air-lift,
bubble column etc.); Instrumentation and control in bioreactors; Aeration and agitation;
Downstream processing (filtration, centrifugation, cell disruption, chromatography, solvent
extraction, membrane separation, drying, lyophilization); Unit operations and bioprocess
economics.

Section 6: Plant, Animal and Microbial Biotechnology
Plants: Totipotency; Regeneration of plants; Plant growth regulators and elicitors; Tissue culture and
cell suspension culture system; Production of secondary metabolites; Genetic engineering of
plants; Agriculture and medicinal biotechnology; Transgenic plants and their applications.
Animals: Culture of animal cells and tissues; Cell lines and their applications; Stem cells and their
applications; Genetic engineering of animals; Transgenic animals and their applications.
Microbes: Production of organic acids, solvents, enzymes and antibiotics; Biofuels; Bioremediation;
Biosensors; Microbial transformation; Biopesticides and biofertilizers.
Section 7: Recombinant DNA Technology and Other Tools in Biotechnology
Recombinant DNA technology: Restriction and modification enzymes; Vectors; DNA and RNA
labeling and sequencing; PCR; cDNA and genomic DNA library; Gene isolation and cloning;
Expression of recombinant proteins in various hosts; DNA fingerprinting; Southern and northern
blotting; Gene-delivery systems in plants and animals; Gene knock-out and gene knock-in; Site
directed mutagenesis; Gene silencing.
Section 8: Bioinformatics and Computational Biology
Computational biology: Data mining and analytical tools for genomics and proteomics; DNA and
protein sequence alignment; Structural bioinformatics (protein structure prediction, molecular
modeling).
