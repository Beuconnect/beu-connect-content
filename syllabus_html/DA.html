GATE 2026 IIT Guwahati | Organizing Institute
DA Data Science and Artificial Intelligence
Probability and Statistics: Counting (permutation and combinations), probability axioms, Sample
space, events, independent events, mutually exclusive events, marginal, conditional and joint
probability; Bayes Theorem, conditional expectation and variance, mean, median, mode and
standard deviation; Random variables, discrete random variables and probability mass functions,
uniform, Bernoulli, binomial distribution, Continuous random variables and probability
distribution functions, uniform, exponential, Poisson, normal, standard normal, t-distribution,
chi-squared distributions, cumulative distribution function; Conditional and joint distributions,
Expectation, variance, conditional expectation and variance, joint and conditional expectation and
variance; Correlation and covariance, transformations of random variables, central limit theorem.
Linear Algebra: Vector space, subspaces, linear dependence and independence of vectors, matrices,
projection matrix, orthogonal matrix, idempotent matrix, partition matrix and their properties,
quadratic forms, systems of linear equations and their solutions; Gaussian elimination,
eigenvalues and eigenvectors, determinant, rank, nullity, projections, LU decomposition,
singular value decomposition.
Calculus and Optimization: Functions of a single variable, limit, continuity and
differentiability, Taylor series, maxima and minima, optimization involving a single variable;
Functions of several variables, partial derivatives, gradient, directional derivatives, total
derivative, Hessian, chain rule, expansion of functions of several variables, local maxima and
minima, saddle point, linear and non-linear programming, unconstrained and constrained
optimization, Lagrange multipliers.
Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms: Programming in Python; Python basics,
control structures, functions, object-oriented programming, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib;
Searching and sorting, hashing, recursion; Basic data structures; stacks, queues, linked lists,
trees, binary search trees; Algorithm design techniques: greedy, dynamic programming and
divide‐and‐conquer; Graph traversals, minimum spanning trees, shortest paths.
Database Management and Warehousing: ER‐model, Relational model: relational algebra,
tuple calculus, SQL; Integrity constraints, normal forms, file organization, indexing, data
warehousing, data mining.
Machine Learning: Supervised Learning: Regression and classification problems, simple linear
regression, multiple linear regression, ridge regression, lasso regression, logistic regression,
k-nearest neighbor, naive Bayes classifier, linear discriminant analysis, support vector
machines, decision trees, random forest, boosting, bagging, neural networks, deep learning;
Unsupervised Learning: clustering, k-means, hierarchical clustering, principal component
analysis; Association rules.
Artificial Intelligence: Search: Informed search, uninformed search, adversarial search; Logic:
propositional logic, first order logic; Reasoning under uncertainty: conditional independence
representation, exact inference through variable elimination, approximate inference through
sampling.
